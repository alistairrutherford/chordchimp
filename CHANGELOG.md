# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-18)

The first version published as a GitHub release, as a universal macOS zip (signed ad hoc, not
notarized).

### Added

- **Info View:** a strip along the bottom of the editor, like Live's own Info View. Point at any
  control, chord pad, effects card or the keyboard and it shows what it does and what its settings
  mean; elsewhere, a short hint. Heard and Export show what they hold now (the key heard, the bars
  ready, or why not). It replaces the tooltips.
- The editor is 64 px taller to make room: 780 × 624 by default, 720 × 584 at its smallest.
- The README's screenshot shows the editor with the Info View.

## 1.0.0

The first version: every white key plays a full chord and every black key a borrowed one, with presets, chord effects, Analyse (key detection) and Export. Not published as a release.
